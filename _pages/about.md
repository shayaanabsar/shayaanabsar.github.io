I am an early-career Machine Learning Researcher and Engineer in my penultimate year studying for a **BSc (Hons) in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science** at the [University of Edinburgh](https://www.ed.ac.uk/).

My work centers on **multilingual Natural Language Processing**, with a particular focus on low-resource language technologies, cross-lingual transfer, and foundation model calibration.

---

### Research Interests

- **Low-Resource & Endangered Languages**: Developing tokenization-free, byte-level frameworks (ByT5) and inductive transfer pipelines to preserve and revitalize underrepresented languages (Quechua, Northern Sámi, Cherokee).
- **Code-Switching & Linguistic Inclusivity**: Quantifying intra-sentential language alternation (the _S-Index_) and fine-tuning cross-lingual representations for multilingual communities.
- **Model Evaluation & Calibration**: Benchmarking commercial and open LLM reasoning capabilities and analyzing confidence calibration versus raw decision accuracy.
