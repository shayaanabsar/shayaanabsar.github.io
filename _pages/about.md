I am an early-career Machine Learning Researcher and Engineer in my penultimate year studying for a BSc (Hons) in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science at the [University of Edinburgh](https://www.ed.ac.uk/).

My research primarily focuses on **multilingual natural language processing**, specifically cross-lingual transfer learning, morphological processing, and representation learning for low-resource and endangered languages. I am motivated by developing NLP systems that are linguistically inclusive and accessible to underrepresented language communities worldwide. In parallel, I am interested in foundation model architectures, evaluation methodologies, and model calibration.

Previously, I was an Undergraduate Research Intern funded by the **Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC)**, supervised by Prof. Oisin Mac Aodha and collaborating with researchers from Google DeepMind, Cambridge, and EPFL. I also worked as a Natural Language Processing Research Assistant at the **University of Edinburgh Business School**, developing pipelines for semantic and thematic classification across corporate earnings call corpora.

Beyond research, I serve as the **NLP Lead** at [EdinburghAI](https://edinburghai.org/) (organizing hands-on ML workshops for a 500+ member student community) and as the Diversity Officer for School of Informatics Rugby.
